Every deed is an NFT that owns a running paid API — a vault with its treasury, price list, customer ledger and worker key. Buy the deed and the business is yours, in one transaction.

The cashier can only charge — its key can never withdraw. A listing locks the vault — the buyer sees exactly what runs. The sale rotates the worker — deliveries survive, no downtime.
