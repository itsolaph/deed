A paid API you can hold

Every deed is an NFT that owns a transferable service instance: a running paid API's vault with its treasury, price list, customer ledger and worker key. Buy the deed and the instance is yours, in one transaction. Hosting stays off chain.

  • The cashier can only charge. Its key can never withdraw.
  • A listing locks the vault. The buyer sees exactly what runs.
  • The sale rotates the worker. Open receipts move to the new one.
